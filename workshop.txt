title=[B42] Peek a View
description=[h1]Expand your vision[/h1]
description=
description=Project Zomboid hides a lot of what your character can actually see behind walls, trees, and floors above. PeekAView extends three rendering passes so your screen catches up with your character's eyes:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Wall cutaway[/b]: walls are cut away at greater range, so corners and doorways reveal what's behind them earlier as you approach.
description=[*][b]Tree fade[/b]: while driving, trees around your vehicle become translucent for earlier hazard visibility.
description=[*][b]Stair view[/b]: the upper floor renders while you're climbing, so you see what's upstairs before you reach it. Zombies reveal only in the room you're climbing into.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Same rules as vanilla: no X-ray, no see-through walls, no enemy outlines. Your character's line of sight still decides what's visible.
description=
description=Each feature has its own Enable toggle under Options → Mods → Peek a View. [b]F8[/b] toggles the whole mod in-game (rebindable).
description=
description=Since build 42.20, PZ ships its own on-foot tree fade. It does the job in my book, so PeekAView's tree fade now covers driving only. If TIS reworks theirs, or I spot room for improvement while playing stable myself, I'll revisit.
description=

description=[h1]FAQ[/h1]
description=
description=[b]What does "Fix B42 Wall-Hiding Bug" do?[/b]
description=Vanilla B42 can hide upper-floor walls next to player-built structures, leaving wall holes from certain angles. The toggle (off by default) suppresses that. Trade-off: player-built decks attached to vanilla walls render more visibly at the facade, so enable it only if you actually see the holes.
description=
description=[b]I declined PeekAView in the ZombieBuddy prompt. Can I undo it?[/b]
description=Edit [noparse]%USERPROFILE%\.zombie_buddy\mod_approvals.json[/noparse]: in the [noparse]"id": "PeekAView"[/noparse] block, flip [noparse]"decision": false[/noparse] to [noparse]true[/noparse] (or delete the block to be re-asked). Restart PZ.
