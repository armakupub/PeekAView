[h1]What it does[/h1]

Your character sees more than your screen shows. Walls hide the corner they're staring down, a tree crown covers the zombie on the road, and a staircase is a blind climb into whatever waits at the top. PeekAView closes that gap in three places:

[list]
[*][b]Wall cutaway[/b]: walls start cutting away at greater distance, so doorways and corners open up as you approach instead of at the last step.
[*][b]Tree fade[/b]: trees between you and where you're looking turn see-through, on foot and behind the wheel.
[*][b]Stair view[/b]: the upper floor draws while you climb, so you see what's waiting before you get there. Zombies in the room you're climbing into stay visible through the whole climb, anywhere in that room.
[/list]

To be clear: no wallhack. Your character's line of sight still decides everything, the mod only draws what the game already counts as seen but keeps hidden.

Every feature has its own toggle under Options → Mods → Peek a View, and [b]F8[/b] switches the whole mod on and off in game (rebindable).

About the vanilla tree fade: build 42.20 added one, and it's decent. Mine replaces it with whole-tree fading that follows your view direction and knows how big each tree actually is, without the dithered circle around your character. If you'd rather keep the vanilla version, untick "Active on foot" and PeekAView will only fade trees while you drive.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]

[b]What does "Fix B42 Wall-Hiding Bug" do?[/b]
Vanilla B42 can hide upper-floor walls next to player-built structures, leaving wall holes from certain angles. The toggle (off by default) suppresses that. Trade-off: player-built decks attached to vanilla walls render more visibly at the facade, so enable it only if you actually see the holes.

[b]I declined PeekAView in the ZombieBuddy prompt. Can I undo it?[/b]
Edit [noparse]%USERPROFILE%\.zombie_buddy\mod_approvals.json[/noparse]: in the [noparse]"id": "PeekAView"[/noparse] block, flip [noparse]"decision": false[/noparse] to [noparse]true[/noparse] (or delete the block to be re-asked). Restart PZ.
